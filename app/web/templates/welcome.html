One ingest, one analysis, then every format generated and checked on its own bench. A format that fails does not stop the others.

How it works

1

Read once

The source is hashed, extracted, normalised and cut into chunks with permanent ids. No model touches this step, so the text an artefact cites is the text you supplied.

2

Understand once

A single structured pass classifies the source and its provenance. The result is cached on the job and shared by every generator, so seven formats cost one analysis.

3

Write, then check

Each format is drafted against the chunks and put in front of six checkers. Anything that fails comes back with a specific fix note and is rewritten, up to three times.

What comes out

Why you can publish what it writes

Every claim cites its chunk

Not a bibliography at the end — each factual statement carries the id of the passage it came from.

Six checkers, independently

Grounding, format, tone, safety, editorial and source reuse. Two are deterministic and provably make no model call.

It fails closed

A checker that could not run withholds the artefact as unverified rather than letting it through.

No model in the ingest path

Extraction and chunking are deterministic code. Your source is never quietly rewritten before it is read.

Put something through it

A page of text is enough to see the whole pipeline run.
